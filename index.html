Find work worth doing. Discover local and remote projects where your skills can make a difference.

What we're building

Project Work is a proposed discovery platform connecting people who need help with people who can get projects done—from repairs, moving, and landscaping to custom fabrication, sewing, and creative work.

Discover opportunities

Browse relevant project requests by location, category, and keywords.

Built around real skills

Help independent workers and makers find projects that fit what they do best.

Current development stage

We are evaluating authorized ways to discover and display publicly shared project requests. Project Work is not yet open to users. Any future third-party content integration will depend on the relevant platform's approval and requirements.
